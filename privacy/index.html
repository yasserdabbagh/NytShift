Privacy note

Last updated 19 November 2026

This note covers the waitlist on nytshift.com. NytShift is in private development and this page will be replaced by a full privacy policy before the product launches.

Who is responsible

NytShift. Contact: hello@nytshift.com.

What we keep

  • Your email address.
  • Which language you were reading the page in.
  • The date and time you joined.

Our web server also records your IP address for security, as all web servers do. Those records are deleted after about two weeks. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Why

Only to tell you when the first places open. We don't sell your address or share it with anyone, and we won't send you marketing in the meantime.

Where it is stored

On our own server, hosted by Hetzner in Germany. When someone joins, we get a short alert on WhatsApp saying that someone joined. The alert doesn't include your address.

How long

Until we launch and contact you, and no longer than 12 months after that. Or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. If we haven't launched by 31 December 2027, we delete the whole list.

Your choices

Email hello@nytshift.com to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can also ask us to take you off the waitlist at any time.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies and uses no trackers or analytics. If you switch language, your browser remembers the choice on your own device.

From software to a workforce

SaaS

For twenty years, business software has meant SaaS. You pay a monthly fee, you get a tool, and someone on your team still has to sit down and use it.

AaaS

Agentic as a Service (AaaS) is the next step. You subscribe to agents that do the work themselves. They plan, write, design, reply and follow up, then bring the finished work to you for a yes. You direct them the way you'd direct a team, without hiring anyone.

02 //

What the workforce will do

01Plans.

Builds each week's content around what you sell and what your customers respond to, and changes course when the numbers say so.

02Creates.

Posts, carousels, short videos and captions, in Arabic and English, made from your own photos and your brand.

03Repliesin build

Reads comments and messages, picks out the real enquiries, and drafts a reply for you to approve.

04Reports.

One page a week: what went out, what worked, and what the workforce did for you.

03 //

How a week runs

The work happens while you're busy with everything else. Once a week you sit down, look over what's finished, and approve it in one go. Nothing reaches your customers without your tap.

Setup works the same way. An onboarding agent asks you about your business, then you connect your accounts through Meta's official sign-in. There's no call to book and no new software to learn.

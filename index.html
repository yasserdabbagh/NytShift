From software to a workforce

SaaSYou do the work

Most business software today is software as a service (SaaS). You pay a monthly fee, you get a tool, and someone on your team still has to sit down and use it.

AaaSWork arrives done, you approve
Approve

Agentic as a Service (AaaS) is the next step. You subscribe to agents that do the work themselves: they plan, write, design and draft replies, then bring you the finished work for a yes. You direct them the way you'd direct a team, without hiring anyone.

How a week will run

PlanCreateApprove

The work will get done while you're busy with everything else. Once a week you'll sit down, look over what's finished and approve it in one go. Nothing reaches your customers without your tap.

Setup will work the same way. An onboarding agent will ask you about your business, then you'll connect your accounts through each platform's official sign-in. No call to book, no new software to learn.

01 / The concept

While the world sleeps, the work happens.

Most knowledge work waits for morning. A procurement comparison sits until somebody opens the file. A report sits until somebody starts writing. A review sits until somebody has time.

Nytshift is the workforce that doesn't wait. You hand off the brief at the end of the day. Agents reason, draft, analyze, and assemble through the night. The output is ready before the first coffee.

Not assistance. Not augmentation. The work itself, done.

02 / The work

What gets done between dusk and dawn.

Built first for the construction and industrial sector. The categories of work are deliberate, narrow, and chosen for repeatability.

01
Procurement comparison
Vendor offers analyzed, normalized, costed, and risk flagged. Detailed comparison sheets that used to take days, prepared overnight.
02
Document drafting and review
Contracts, technical specifications, scopes of work. Drafted, redlined, and structured against your standards.
03
Reporting and synthesis
Operational data turned into briefings, dashboards, and decision ready summaries. Ready for the morning meeting.
04
Exception review
Sites, schedules, and ledgers scanned for the anomalies a human would catch on a long enough day. Flagged before they compound.
